# Electric Biochar Toilet

A small electric cooker, built from a converted 8-gallon stainless brewing fermenter and two cartridge heaters, that dries and torrefies human and organic waste into sterile, lightweight char.

**See the full project: https://mars-doom.github.io/biochar-toilet/**

Made by Charlie Britton at the Mars College Dept of Makers, 2027.
